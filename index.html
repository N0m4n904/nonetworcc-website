Max.Storage Ultimate for Home Assistant

A Home Assistant integration in Python for the MaxStorage Max.Storage Ultimate home battery. It exposes considerably more than the official Modbus interface: live photovoltaic, battery and grid readings, dynamically discovered MPPT strings, and relay states, which makes surplus energy usable in automations. Installable through HACS.

A substantially extended fork of geeks-r-us/maxstorage_ultimate.

Smart Cursor

A custom pointer for the web: a glowing dot and a ring that morphs onto whatever it hovers, matching that element's position, size and corner radius, and yielding to a caret over text. It reads the brightness of the surface underneath to stay legible on light and dark pages alike, and every dimension it draws is a CSS custom property. Two files, no build step, no dependencies.

It is what is drawing your pointer on this page.
