II. The rights of users and data subjects
With regard to the data processing to be described in more detail below, users and data subjects have the right

Likewise, under Art. 21 GDPR, users and data subjects have the right to object to the controller's future processing of their data pursuant to Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f) GDPR. In particular, an objection to data processing for the purpose of direct advertising is permissible.

1. Instagram content

This website shows selected posts from the Instagram account @b9_allroad_kru, operated by Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.

These posts are NOT loaded when you open the page. In their place you are shown a button. Only when you press one of these buttons is the post requested from instagram.com. Nothing is transmitted to Meta before that point.

Once you press such a button, your browser connects to Meta's servers. Meta thereby receives your IP address, information about your browser and operating system, the address of the page you are on, and the time of the request, and may store and read cookies on your device. If you are logged in to Instagram at that moment, Meta can associate the visit with your account. This may involve the transfer of data to servers outside the European Union.

The legal basis for this processing is your consent under Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. a) GDPR, which you give by pressing the button. You may withhold it simply by not pressing it, and no part of this website depends on doing so. Consent already given applies only to the current page view: reloading the page returns every post to its unloaded state. Cookies already set by Meta can be removed through your browser settings.

We have no influence over the extent of the data collected by Meta, nor over how it is processed thereafter. For information on that, please refer to the privacy policy of Instagram at https://privacycenter.instagram.com/policy.
